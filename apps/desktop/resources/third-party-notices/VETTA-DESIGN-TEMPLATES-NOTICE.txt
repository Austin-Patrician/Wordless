The built-in design styles that ship as Wordless's style wall — their `theme.css`
token files and `DESIGN.md` specifications — are adapted from the Vetta design
templates catalog (`.vetta/design-templates.json`). That catalog is in turn
adapted from `awesome-design-md` by VoltAgent:

  https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md
  Copyright (c) VoltAgent and contributors
  MIT License

Per-style provenance is kept in the header comment of each `theme.css` file.

Wordless vendors the `theme.css` and `DESIGN.md` resources only. The upstream
`demo.html` worked examples are not shipped: style thumbnails are drawn from the
tokens in `theme.css`, so no per-style document is bundled.

The generated snapshot and the exact transformations applied to the upstream
text are recorded in `src/main/design/style-catalog-upstream.ts` and its
generator, `scripts/sync-design-styles.mjs`.
