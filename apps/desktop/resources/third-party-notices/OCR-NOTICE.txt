OCR (bundled text recognition)
==============================

Wordless ships a local OCR engine so that models which cannot view images can still
read text out of a screenshot or a photo. It runs entirely offline: the ONNX models
are part of the application bundle, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Models: PP-OCRv5 (detection + recognition + dictionary)
- Upstream model family: PaddleOCR (`https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR`)
- Copyright: PaddlePaddle Authors and contributors
- License: Apache-2.0 (SPDX), i.e. the Apache License, Version 2.0
- ONNX conversion: `https://github.com/bent2685/ocr-web` (`@ocr-web/models-ppocrv5`)
- Copyright: ocr-web contributors
- License: MIT

Runtime: onnxruntime-web
- Source: `https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime`
- Copyright: Microsoft Corporation and contributors
- License: MIT

Inference wrapper: `@ocr-web/core`
- Source: `https://github.com/bent2685/ocr-web`
- Copyright: ocr-web contributors
- License: MIT

The models are downloaded at package time and verified against a locked SHA-256. The
wasm and its JavaScript glue are copied from the installed `onnxruntime-web` package so
that both halves are always the same version.
